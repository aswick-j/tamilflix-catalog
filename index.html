TamilFlix

A Tamil-language catalog addon for compatible Stremio-protocol clients such as Nuvio.

Catalogs: Tamil movies, Tamil series, manually curated Tamil-dubbed titles, and a Tamil live-TV channel directory.

Catalog and metadata only. No video streams or live-TV playback links are provided.
